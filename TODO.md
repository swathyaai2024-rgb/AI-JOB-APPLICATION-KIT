# CareerCraft AI — Delivery Checklist

## Trust-focused landing page
- Present CareerCraft AI as an honest job application kit for students and early-career applicants.
- Explain that AI supports drafting and review but does not replace human judgment.
- Surface privacy posture, server-side API-key protection, and claim verification.
- Include clear calls to action into the interactive demo.

## Interactive no-account demo
- Provide lightweight client-side navigation across Resume Lab, Cover Letter Generator, ATS-Style Analyzer, Truth Checker, Portfolio Builder, Self-Intro Script Generator, and Before-and-After Comparison.
- Use realistic fictional student content and label the experience as a capstone demonstration.
- Keep the experience responsive and keyboard-accessible on desktop and mobile.

## Career-kit tools
- Resume Lab preserves the student's real experience and prompts human review.
- Cover Letter Generator creates an editable sample without inventing qualifications.
- ATS-Style Analyzer covers keyword alignment, formatting, readability, and role relevance without claiming to reproduce a real ATS score.
- Truth Checker highlights unsupported or unverifiable claims and asks the user to confirm or revise them.
- Portfolio Builder previews projects, skills, outcomes, and links in a polished early-career page.
- Self-Intro Script Generator creates an editable concise introduction for interviews, networking, and career fairs.
- Before-and-After Comparison shows original and AI-assisted content side by side with visible changes and review prompts.

## Delivery
- Serve a static website on the configured port.
- Provide a valid /manus-routes.json manifest.
- Validate source, runtime, manifest, and responsive layout before checkpointing.
